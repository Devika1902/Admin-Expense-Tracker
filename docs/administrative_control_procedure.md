# Administrative Expense Control Procedure

## Purpose

Use this procedure to review a travel declaration, compare it with the simulated ERP cost extract, record document follow-up and prepare a project-spend view. This is a training procedure; follow the organization's approved policy and system of record for real transactions.

## Review sequence

1. **Confirm the source period.** Note the report date and period covered. Keep the original employee declarations and ERP export unchanged; work from copies.
2. **Check master data.** Confirm the employee ID exists and the project code is active and appropriate for the trip. Review department, manager and cost centre where relevant.
3. **Check completeness.** Verify travel start/end dates, expense category, declared amount and receipt reference. Record each missing item as a follow-up against its stable travel reference.
4. **Reconcile by reference and dimensions.** Pair on travel reference first. Confirm project, employee/vendor reference and category before considering amount. Treat an equal amount alone as insufficient evidence.
5. **Review exceptions.** Investigate amount and dimension discrepancies, unmatched declarations, ERP-only transactions and potential duplicates against source documents. Record the explanation and disposition with the record.
6. **Manage follow-ups.** Assign an owner, send a clear request, record the request and due dates, and update status and resolution date. Follow the organization's escalation timeline for overdue items.
7. **Prepare management reporting.** Summarize recorded spend by project and department. Compare spend with approved budgets and explain material variances. Keep unresolved items visible in the report.
8. **Retain evidence.** Store the report, source extracts, clarifications and review outcome in the approved location with the required access controls and retention period.

## Exception dispositions

- **Exact match:** all available dimensions agree and the absolute amount difference is at most EUR 0.01. This is a matching result, not an approval decision.
- **Amount discrepancy:** dimensions align but recorded and declared amounts differ by more than EUR 0.01. Review tax, currency, split postings, credits and timing before disposition.
- **Dimension discrepancy:** project, employee/reference or category differs. Confirm the source and correct the coding through the authorized process.
- **Unmatched declaration / unmatched ERP transaction:** investigate missing references, timing differences, cancellations and unrelated project costs. Do not force a match based only on amount.
- **Potential duplicate:** compare receipt, travel dates, merchant and original submission. A duplicate flag is a prompt for human review and may be a legitimate repeated charge.

## Data handling notes

All supplied records are synthetic. The generated date window and EUR amounts are for practice only. Never place real personal or financial records in this sample project unless authorized and stored in an approved environment.
